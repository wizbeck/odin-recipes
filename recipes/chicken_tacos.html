Chicken Tacos

Description

Delicious easy recipe for chicken tacos, on Taco Tuesday! Quick simple and fun dinner or lunch for couples or small families.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 6-8 soft taco size tortillas
  • 1/2 cup red onion
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • Your choice of salsa or hot sauce as topping
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil (avocado or olive oil)
  • Chicken seasonings:
    • 1 tsp Ancho or Chipotle Pepper chili powder
    • 1 tsp Salt
    • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • Optional: Lime to squeeze over tacos

Steps

  1. Preheat pan to medium-high heat to cook chicken.
  2. Prepare chicken in separate bowl or cutting board.
  3. Sprinkle chicken seasonings over the chicken and rub into the chicken.
  4. Once pan has reached temperature, add cooking oil. Let the cooking oil get a hot, but not up to full pan temperature.
  5. Place chicken in pan to begin cooking.
  6. While chicken is cooking, dice the red onion, and chop the cilantro.
  7. If desired preheat another pan to heat up tortillas, when chicken is ready.
  8. When chicken is browned (or has a char to it, depending on preference) and interal temp of the chicken has reached 165°F, turn off the burner.
  9. Optional: Heat up tortillas, until very slightly browned. Keeping soft.
  10. Remove chicken from pan and cut into long, thin strips.
  11. On a plate, place three tortillas laid out flat.
  12. Add chicken, onion, and cliantro.
  13. Top with a splash of favorite hot sauce, salsa, and/or fresh lime.
  14. Enjoy!
