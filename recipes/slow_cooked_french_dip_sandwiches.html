Slow Cooker French Dip Sandwiches

Description

Delicious conveninent French Dip Sandwich that can be done using a slow cooker to set and forget until dinner time! This recipe makes 4 sandwiches, increment ingredients as needed. Can be served with a side of fries as additional dipping food when you run out of sandwich and there is still dipping broth left.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs of rump roast
  • 4 medium french bread rolls
  • ½ (12 fl oz.) can or bottle of beer
  • ½ (10.5 oz.) can condensed French onion soup
  • ½ (10.5 oz.) can beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Steps

  1. Add rump roast to the slow cooker. (Optional: trim excess fat from the rump roast before adding to the slow cooker)
  2. Pour in the liquid ingredients: Beef broth, beer, and the condensed french onion soup.
  3. Place the lid on the slow cooker, and cook the rump roast on low heat for 7 hours.
  4. As it gets closer to dinner time. Preheat the oven to 350 °F
  5. Slice the French bread rolls length-wise down the middle, taking care not to completely sever the top from the bottom half.
  6. Spread butter inside the frech bread rolls.
  7. Bake them in oven for 10 minutes until heated through, or the desired toastiness is reached.
  8. While bread is baking, remove the rump roast from the slow cooker, and let rest for a few minutes before cutting.
  9. Thinly slice the rump roast with the grain, diagonally to get nice easy cuts of the roast.
  10. Use the liquid mixture from the slow cooker as dipping sauce and pour into a few small bowls for dipping.
  11. Pile slices into the bread rolls, and serve.
  12. Optional: Add cheese to the sandwiches for extra yumminess.
  13. Enjoy!
