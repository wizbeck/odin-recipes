Avocado Toast

Description

Avocado Toast is a quick and easy recipe for when you need a hearty breakfast on the go. It takes no time to prepare, especially if you are running late in the morning. Highly recommended by milennials who have a long commute and need a quick breakfast in the mornings.

Ingredients

  • 2 Slices of bread of your choice
  • 1 Avocado
  • Seasonings to taste (Salt, and smoke chili powder are my go-to seasonings

Steps

  1. Toast your bread to desired toastiness.
  2. While your bread is toasting you can cut and prepare the avocado for topping just as the toast becomes ready. To cut your avocado, use a knife to cut in half vertical-wise, but not all the way through, you'll want to cut around the avocado, and avoid the pit. Once the cut connects to the other end of the avocado you should be able to twist it apart, leaving the pit in one side. Then, using the knife strike the pit with the blade, with a light amount of force to lodge the blade into the pit. Twist the knife to dislodge pit from avocado, for easy removal.
  3. Using a fork, mash each side of the avocado flesh, but take care not to destroy the outer skin. The avocado should mashed softly into a chunky cream texture for each avocado half.
  4. Avocado is ready. When toast is finished, squeeze out the chunky avocado onto each piece of toast. One side of avocado per toast. That should be enough to cover each piece of toast generously.
